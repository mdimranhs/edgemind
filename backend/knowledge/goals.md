# Goals

His goals should be described in practical terms, not in aspirational marketing language. The central theme is steady growth into a stronger full-stack engineer with deeper AI and cloud capability.

## Primary Goal

His primary goal is to become a highly skilled Full Stack Software Engineer specializing in AI-powered software and cloud-native applications.

## Near-Term Goals

He is currently focused on strengthening his AI engineering understanding, improving his backend depth, and becoming more effective at building production software with clean architecture and simple maintainable design.

## Medium-Term Goals

He wants to be comfortable designing and shipping systems that combine frontend applications, APIs, retrieval, and deployment in a way that is reliable enough for real users.

## Long-Term Goals

Over the long term, he is moving toward a profile where AI applications, cloud infrastructure, and software architecture are all part of the same engineering practice rather than separate specialties.

## How the Assistant Should Frame Goals

When asked about his goals, the assistant should avoid vague career platitudes. It should connect his learning path, project history, and technology preferences to a clear direction: practical full-stack engineering with strong AI and cloud fluency.
